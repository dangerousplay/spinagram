description: Zone headers that edges avoid, centred icons clear of the text, rows placed inside zones, snakes, and smaller SVGs.

These notes describe the next release, 0.5, which is not published yet. Install spinagram from the repository to
try it.

spinagram 0.5 takes on the slides that still needed hand-placed coordinates in 0.4: phase bands, cards laid out
inside a zone, processes that wrap onto several rows, and edges that cut through zone headers. You declare the
zone and the steps; the engine places the cards and routes the edges.

![A delivery pipeline wrapping through two phase zones, two banded zones with rows placed for you, a zone anchor and an elbow edge](/assets/release-0-5/commit-to-production.svg)

The source is `docs/diagrams/release_0_5.py`. It places no card by hand: only the snake, the bands and the zone widths have coordinates.

## Edges stay off zone headers

`route="ortho"` routes around zone headers, as it routes around boxes. `check` reports a straight edge that
crosses a header as `edge-crosses-zone-header` (P0), and `check --fix` moves its anchors to the first pair of
side fractions that clears every header, or switches it to `route: ortho`. A header can also sit on the right
(`header="right"`) or be left out (`header="none"`).

## Centred icons clear of the text

A centred icon reserves its height: the text starts 8 px below it, and the icon shrinks to 24 px before the
text does (`defaults.node.icon_text_gap`, `icon_min`). `check` reports an icon over its own text as
`icon-overlaps-text` (P0) and fixes it with a taller box. `align="auto"` picks a centred icon for tall cards
and a left icon for wide ones.

## Zones that hold rows

```python
with d.zone("Observe", band=(560, 790)):
    d.row(["Metrics", "Logs", "Traces"], cols=3)
```

`band=(y0, y1)` spans a zone across the safe width. A row or column inside it needs no coordinates: the engine
places it below the header, centred or at the `start` or `end`, and `cols=N` gives each card 1/N of the width.
`d.row(items)` with no size returns the nodes. Edges reach a point of a zone's outline with `z.top`,
`z.at_top(0.8)` and the other side helpers.

## Snakes

```python
d.snake(steps, cols=4, y=170, h=100, zones=["Build", "Release"])
```

A long process wraps onto rows of `cols` cards, each row in its own zone if you want, chained by edges that
turn between rows clear of the headers. Rows alternate direction (`mode="boustrophedon"`) or all run left to
right (`mode="restart"`). YAML: `snakes:`.

## Even text in a row

`same_text_scale=True` on a row or column gives every member the same title and body size, so one long title
does not leave its card with smaller text. Without it, `check` reports sizes that differ by more than 10 % as
`text-scale-mismatch`.

## Helpers

- `d["api"]` returns a node or zone by id.
- `d.distribute(n, w, gap, within=(x0, x1))` returns the x of `n` boxes centred in a span, or equal widths that
  fill it.
- `route="elbow"` with `mid=` draws an edge with two bends at a chosen x or y.
- `origin="safe"` measures coordinates from the canvas margin, so `x=0, y=0` is the safe area's corner.

## Themes

- **By name.** `--theme acme` also looks in each `SPINAGRAM_THEME_PATH` directory and in installed packages
  that register a `spinagram.themes` entry point. The error lists every place it searched.
- **Glyph map.** `text: {glyph_map: {"→": "->"}}` replaces characters the brand font lacks, in facts too;
  `check` lists each one as `glyph-mapped`.
- **Vendor icons.** `check` reports a generic icon on a box that names a cloud service
  (`icon-generic-for-service`), and `icons: {prefer_vendor_for_services: true}` swaps it for the vendor icon.

## Smaller SVGs

SVGs now embed only the characters they draw from each font. `render --fonts embed` keeps whole font files
(the 0.4 output), and `--fonts link` embeds none and imports Google Fonts instead. In Python, use
`to_svg(scene, fonts=...)` or `spinagram.render(..., backend_options={"svg": {"fonts": "link"}})`.

## Also in 0.5

- `zone-underused` counts nested zones as content, so a Region that holds a VPC is not reported.
- `check --fix` on a Python spec with `origin="safe"` prints label positions in the spec's coordinates.
